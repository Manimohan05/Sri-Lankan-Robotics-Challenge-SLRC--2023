# Sri Lankan Robotics Challenge (SLRC) 2023

**Robotics & Automation · Team competition · 2023**

Our team's robot for the **Sri Lankan Robotics Challenge 2022/23**, the 10th edition of the national robotics competition organised by the E-Club of the Department of Electronic & Telecommunication Engineering, University of Moratuwa.

The competition tests strategic problem solving and robot design. We designed and built an autonomous 4WD robot with a mounted robot arm, modelled first in CAD and then built as a physical robot.

## Robot Design
- **Chassis:** 4WD smart-car / Arduino buggy platform
- **Manipulator:** robot arm mounted on the chassis
- **Control:** Arduino firmware for motor control (L293D driver) and colour sensing, with motor circuits simulated in Proteus
- **CAD:** SolidWorks / STEP / STL models of the chassis, arm and full assembly
